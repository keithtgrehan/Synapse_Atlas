## Explicit unresolved questions and escalation

1. **Row 2:** 184 vs 181/169/166/121 participant denominators. Preserve provenance; trial-methodology/publication clarification needed only for harmonized counts or population metrics, not the narrow mapping.
2. **Row 4:** 598 registry vs 602 publication randomized. No inferred reconciliation; data-curation or trial-author clarification if merging denominator fields.
3. **Row 9:** `healthyVolunteers=true` contradicts patient-specific PTSD eligibility and reported study population. Keep both raw flag and explanatory warning; registry data-stewardship input if correcting classification.
4. **Rows 1, 5, 8:** historical affiliations/funders differ from captured lead sponsor; only corporate/legal specialist evidence may support ownership or succession claims, none of which are approved here.
5. **Rows 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6:** chemical synonyms, salts, and formulation equivalence have **not** been independently verified; no chemical identity assertion is approved. For explicit equivalence claims, request chemistry/CMC source evidence.
6. **Evidence limitation:** the original JSON payloads and SHA-256 hashes could not be independently reproduced from the attached Markdown alone; the manuscript full texts were not all available for independent Methods verification. Before portraying these as independently audited, attach/fetch the original exact JSON responses and check the cited publication's relevant NCT registration/Methods section.
